# J11 Attach and reassess evidence

## Preconditions

Use synthetic fixtures and isolated stores. Record code/spec revisions, runtime versions, actual actors, and the relevant configuration. Milestone membership is defined in catalog.json.

## Acceptance criteria

- **J11-A:** Attach a reproducible evidence record to criterion and revisions with outcome and protected artifact reference.
- **J11-B:** Passed, failed, inconclusive, absent, and stale are distinct. A changed spec or relevant code cannot inherit approval silently.
- **J11-C:** Unavailable artifacts retain their known provenance but cannot satisfy current verification without reassessment. Refresh does not rewrite history.

## Evidence

Manifest validation, code/spec change fixtures, permission-aware artifact access, browser distinction of states.

A result must identify which of the criteria above it establishes. Store manifest references; keep private artifacts access-controlled. Unimplemented or unmeasured behavior stays unverified.

## Rules

Applicable invariants: I01, I07. See [quality rules](../../QUALITY.md).

## Completion

All criteria need current evidence against the intended build and requirement revision. Record limitations and open failures in the ledger. Agents evaluate current evidence under QUALITY.md and AUTONOMY.md and advance when criteria pass; no human acceptance is required.
